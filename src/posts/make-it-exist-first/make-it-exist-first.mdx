# Make it Exist First

There's a good chance you have some sort of ⭐ideas⭐ folder, paper, journal, or that, maybe, you keep them all in your head.
Whatever the case, among those brilliant ideas, there are probably some that you haven't touched in months or years.

<Align align="center">
_Why haven't you touched them?_
</Align>
Well, maybe those ideas are _"not good enough"_, or maybe they're _"too big"_ to get started with them. 
Maybe you already started working on them, but you just can't get them to that sweet spot in which you're finally happy to show them to the world.

"Just a little more work until they're _perfect_."

<Media label="Image Credits: growthbyvisuals" width={360}>
  <img src={meme} alt="Make it Exist First" />
</Media>

I've been finding myself in this situation for some time. My [youtube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@cashewolddew) is an especially obvious case.
I have a folder with over 50 video ideas, partial scripts, even fully completed scripts. Yet, they've somehow never seen the light of day. 

_Why?_

_In my mind_ it is because those videos need to, at least, respect a minimum quality standard. 
I do not expect them to be perfect, but I am aware that I would waste my watchers' time if I were to upload an unedited, long, and not so well thought video.
Add the friction of thumbnails, descriptions, tags, links, documentation, and no wonder things are the way they are.

<Separator />

Alright, but that just means that I should pick, at least some of my ideas to turn into reality, accept that others are doomed to fail, and move on...

>So long, video idea in which I discuss the weird particularities of shaders applied on Godot labels, you had a good run! 
>
>Yes, some game developers might be wasting hours of their lives trying to find a solution to those quirks, but hey, at least you made that _perfect_ video 2 months ago!

Do you see the problem? How many ideas have to die because of the friction or because they're not perfect?

Well, maybe not all ideas have to be perfect..

<Media label="Image Credits: growthbyvisuals" width={360}>
  <img src={meme2} alt="Ugly action beats unfinished perfection" />
</Media>

<Align align="center">
**Just get things out there!**
</ Align>

An idea that reaches 10 people is way more valuable than one that reaches nobody. Zero is not "almost something", it's **nothing**.
When giving up on ideas, you are not compromising quality, you're chosing existance over non-existance.

You're losing the benefit of getting feedback from others. You're losing the time spent on building or thinking about that idea.
Keeping the idea well tucked in its folder only rewards you with _what ifs_ and more self-doubts created by another failed project.

Don't do this! Make is visible! ⭐Your ideas⭐ are worth sharing!

<Separator />

Take this blog for example. I'm not sure of the site's state by the time you read this, but I've been wanting to make an OS-like blog for years.
However, setting up a blog is no easy task, especially considering the OS twist. I just couldn't get started. 
This was doomed to be a project, forever to be pushed back by other more worthy, or smaller projects.

_Why?_

Because in most places you look, you see _perfect_ sites, wonderfully written texts, complexity. Finishing and polishing such a project from scratch could take up months.

So... why finish it? Why not just put it out there? It doesn't have to be perfect, it doesn't need to immediately work for all devices, it doesn't need extraordinary styling. 
It needs to serve a purpose, and I think that for a blog post called 'Make it exist first', it's actually _perfect_ despite being _imperfect_.
